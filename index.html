[02] / Human approval gates

A person approves the action.

Operator is the public human-to-agent control plane. Work is tiered. Reading can proceed. Writing, deploying, deleting, and changing who is allowed to act do not proceed on a casual yes.

  1. 0

    Automatic read

    Status, commits, issues, logs, and tests. Nothing is changed.

  2. 1

    Low-risk

    Research, tests, a branch, or a proposed patch. Still not a merge.

  3. 2

    Human approve

    Write files, commit, open a pull request, staging, or configuration. The gate is an explicit APPROVE.

  4. 3

    Second confirm

    Protected merge, production, delete, secrets, or a change to authority. The gate is CONFIRM.

A reply of “yes” is not approval. Tier 2 and tier 3 wait for APPROVE and CONFIRM with an id. This is the authority model published in the Operator repository. The gateway there is a v0 stub, so this page is not a live console. Bruto Security Gate is the public human-review gate for risky links, files, and attachments.
