AI Reception Supervisor — $199/mo · flat rate · no contract

AI Reception Supervisor

Never miss a call again.

A reception service configured to your specifications, and supervised on your behalf. This is not a phone tree or a generic automated voice. Your AI Reception Supervisor answers as a trained front desk would — designed around your business:

  • A voice you choose — voice, accent, and tone selected by you, so it sounds like your office: warm or direct, formal or relaxed.
  • Trained on your industry — real estate, trades, rentals, claims, and professional services: it learns your services, your prices, your hours, and the language your customers use, so it answers like someone who works there.
  • Your approved words — your greeting and your script. You approve exactly what it says before it takes its first call.
  • Qualifies and books — it does more than take messages: it answers the questions that matter, qualifies the caller, and books the appointment directly into your calendar.
  • Supervised, with you in control — every call summarized for you in writing. Your escalation rules decide what reaches you, and when. You can change any rule or script at any time.

Available 24/7 — nights, weekends, and busy days when calls would otherwise go to voicemail.

$199/month reflects the cost of a dedicated phone line, which is why it is priced above our other monthly systems.
